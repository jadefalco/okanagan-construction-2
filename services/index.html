What We Do

Renovation & Construction Services in Kelowna

Six things we do, all of them with the same crew. Whether it is one room or a whole house, the person you meet at the consultation is the person running your site.

Kitchen Renovations

Layouts reworked, walls opened, cabinetry and stone installed properly. We handle the structure, plumbing, and electrical behind the finishes — not just the finishes.

Learn more

Bathroom Renovations

The smallest room in the house and the least forgiving. Waterproofing and ventilation get sorted first, then the tile, vanity, and glass go on top of a base that will hold.

Learn more

Home Additions

Second storeys, main floor extensions, garages, and suites. An addition succeeds or fails at the seam where new framing meets old, so that’s where we spend our attention.

Learn more

Custom Home Builds

Custom homes on Okanagan slopes, rock, and lakefront lots. Bruce runs the site himself, so the person answering your questions is the same one coordinating the trades.

Learn more

Basement Renovations

The cheapest square footage a house will ever gain — as long as the water, the insulation, and the egress are handled before the drywall goes up.

Learn more

Deck Building

Elevated decks, membrane decks over living space, and covered outdoor living. Framed like structure, because in the Okanagan that is exactly what a deck has to be.

Learn more

Where We Work

Serving Kelowna and the Okanagan Valley

We work throughout Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country, Peachland, Summerland, and Penticton. We are a CHBA member, licensed and insured, and we have been building here since 2005.

If you are not sure which of the above your project falls under, or it spans several of them, just call. Most of the work we do ends up crossing categories anyway.
