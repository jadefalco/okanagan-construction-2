Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our process, timelines, permits, and what to expect when working with our team.

Timelines vary based on scope, but a full home renovation typically ranges from 4 to 12 months. We provide a detailed schedule during the planning phase and keep you informed throughout construction.
